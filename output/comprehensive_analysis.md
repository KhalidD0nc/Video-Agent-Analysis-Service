### Training Tips for the Coach (Based on Data Analysis)

**1. Drill: "3v2 Wide Overload to Finish"**
* **Objective:** To improve the conversion of wide-area possession into high-quality scoring chances, addressing the inefficiency seen in the first half.
* **The Drill:** Set up a 3v2 scenario in the wide channel. The attacking team must use an overlap or underlap to create a crossing opportunity, with specific targets marked in the 6-yard box for the strikers.

**2. Drill: "Direct Vertical Transition Drills"**
* **Objective:** To refine the timing of runs and the accuracy of long-range distribution, mirroring Abha's successful first goal.
* **The Drill:** Start with a contested ball in the middle third. Upon recovery, the player must immediately look for a long diagonal or vertical ball to a striker moving into a marked "scoring zone."

**3. Drill: "Breaking Down the Low Block"**
* **Objective:** To discourage speculative long shots and promote intricate passing through compact defenses.
* **The Drill:** Play 8v10 in the final third. The defense stays in two compact lines of four. The offense scores 2 points for a goal from inside the box and 0 points for any shot taken from outside the 18-yard line.

**4. Drill: "Rest-Defense Awareness Scrimmage"**
* **Objective:** To fix the structural collapse Al Jubail suffered in the central corridor during transitions.
* **The Drill:** During a full-pitch scrimmage, reward the defending team with "bonus points" if they maintain a 2+1 triangle structure (two center-backs and one holding midfielder) while their team is in possession in the opponent's half.

**5. Drill: "Set Piece Specialization: Walls and Runners"**
* **Objective:** To increase set-piece unpredictability and defensive resilience.
* **The Drill:** Practice 10 free kicks from the "D." Five must involve a decoy runner, and five must involve a short lay-off to change the shooting angle before the strike.
